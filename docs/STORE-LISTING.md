## Long description
Clothes should fit you — not the other way round.

Tailor IT connects you with independent fashion designers who make every piece to your measurements.

• **AI virtual try-on** — see designer pieces on your own photo before anything is cut.
• **Your fit profile** — enter measurements once, or let AI estimate a starting point from a photo and fine-tune it yourself.
• **AI stylist** — tell it the occasion; get outfit ideas from real designers, with reasons.
• **Order with confidence** — pay in full or a deposit, track every stage from cutting to delivery, get notified at each step.
• **Private by design** — your tailor only sees your measurements once you've paid. Photos are deleted automatically. Delete your account any time.

Are you a designer? Open your studio: list your collection (with AI-written descriptions), receive orders with exact measurements, manage production and get paid directly.
